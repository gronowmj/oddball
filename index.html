    How to play

    Each day has ten rounds. Every round shows four tiles: pictures or words. Names appear on the pictures once you've tapped.

    Three tiles share a connection. Tap the one that doesn't belong. You get one tap per round, and there's no timer.

    The rounds get harder as you go. At the end you'll see your score and how every round worked.
